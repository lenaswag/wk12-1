✨ Your Reading Adventure

In this story, you will:

🔍 Predict what might happen.

🎧 Choose to read by yourself or listen to the story.

💭 Use clues to understand the characters.

🔄 Check and revise your ideas as you read.

💌 Think about friendship in your own life.

Remember: A prediction does not have to be correct. Good readers use clues and change their thinking when they discover new information!
